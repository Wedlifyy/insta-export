          How to keep the quality after export

          Before you post
          1. In Instagram: Settings → Media quality → Upload at highest quality — turn it on. Without it, Instagram compresses harder on weak connections.
          2. Move the file to your phone without a chat app. WhatsApp and Telegram (as a normal video) recompress it. Use Google Drive, AirDrop, a cable, or open this page on the phone itself.
          3. Upload on strong Wi‑Fi and leave the app open until it finishes.
          4. Skip Instagram's own filters and edits on this clip — they trigger another render.
          Why not just upload 4K?
          • Instagram re-encodes every upload and plays video back at about 1080 pixels wide. Its published limit is 1920 pixels across.
          • A 4K file gets shrunk by Instagram's fast scaler. Shrinking it here first, with a high-quality scaler and plenty of bitrate, hands Instagram a cleaner picture to compress.
          • So the "4K look" on Instagram comes from a sharp 1080p master, not from the pixel count of the upload.
          The spec this tool exports to
          • Container MP4, index at the front of the file (fast start)
          • Video H.264 or HEVC, progressive, 4:2:0, 23–60 fps, up to 1920 px wide, VBR up to 25 Mbps
          • Audio AAC, up to 48 kHz, mono or stereo
          • Reels 3 seconds to 15 minutes, 9:16 recommended. Stories up to 60 seconds.

          From Meta's Instagram Platform documentation (Reel specifications).
